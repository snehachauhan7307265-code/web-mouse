================================================================
WebMouse Helper — Windows Local Companion
================================================================

HOW TO RUN:
1. Double-click "install_helper.bat" to install and set up automatic startup on Windows boot.
   OR double-click "run_helper.bat" to start immediately.

2. A clean window titled "WebMouse Helper — Pair Windows PC" will appear displaying your Pair QR Code.

3. Open WebMouse on your phone and tap:
   [ + Pair Windows PC ]

4. Scan the QR code, tap [ TRUST & CONNECT ], and you're connected!
   Your real Windows mouse cursor will move in real time with your phone finger.

Next time you open WebMouse on your phone, simply tap [ CONNECT ] on your trusted PC card. No QR code or IP entry needed!
